<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user requested a comprehensive set of Agentic Requirements focused on creating a self-healing financial ledger. This involved building four distinct AI agents:

1.  **Fetch Agent:** To autonomously scan connected email inboxes (Gmail/Outlook) and cloud storage (Drive) to find missing invoices (PDFs) that correspond to unreconciled bank transactions.
2.  **Match Agent:** To use predictive logic to automatically suggest Chart of Accounts (COA) mappings when a new subsidiary is connected, flagging anomalies for human review.
3.  **Heal Agent:** To investigate intercompany balance variances by deep-scanning the general ledgers of both entities, find the specific missing transaction, and propose self-healing adjustments.
4.  **Compliance Agent:** To act as a technical validation layer, ensuring intercompany eliminations adhere to governance rules (e.g., only occur between entities in the same control group) and audit standards (IFRS/GAAP).

A critical part of the request was the creation of a Narrative Audit Trail or Logic Memo for every autonomous action, providing transparency and making the system auditable. This includes a Self-Healing Inbox on the dashboard to notify users of agent actions.
